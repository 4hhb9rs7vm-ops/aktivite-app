2. What Data Do We Process?

The App lets you pick what you are doing right now and compare it, as a percentage, with other users. If you wish, you can also see the current moment of friends you've paired with in person by scanning a QR code. For these purposes, the following data is processed:

About moods: Some mood options, such as "I'm sick", may reveal information about your health. Picking these options is entirely optional. If you pick one, it is processed like any other choice: only with your anonymous user ID, only to calculate percentages, and for no longer than 60 minutes. If you have turned on "Let my friends see my moods", the mood you pick is also shown to your paired friends during that time; this option is off by default. By picking such options, you give your explicit consent to this processing.

Before your country, age range, city, gender and university are sent to our server, the App shows you a separate consent screen. If you do not consent, this information is never sent to the server and only the "World" comparison is available.

Camera: The App uses your camera only to scan a friend's QR code in the Friends feature, and only while you have the "Scan code" screen open. Camera images are not recorded or sent to our server; only the content of the QR code is read. The App does not access your location, contacts, photos or microphone. No third-party advertising or analytics trackers are used.

3. How Do We Use Your Data?

Your activity, mood and optional comparison information are used only to calculate and show you the share of other users doing the same activity or in the same mood (for example worldwide, in your country, or among students of your university). In the campus comparison, who is at which university is never shown; only the aggregate percentage is shown. Percentages are calculated from aggregated, anonymous counts created every 15 minutes, not from individual records.

If you use the Friends feature, your paired friends see only your display name and your current moment (your activity; your mood only if mood sharing is on). Your past moments, age, city, country, gender, occupation and university are never shown to your friends.

Your data is never used for advertising, marketing, profiling, or sold to third parties.

4. How Long Is Your Data Kept?

When you pick an activity, your record stays active for 60 minutes and is included in the calculations during that time. Expired records are automatically deleted from the server.

Friends feature data: Your display name and friendship records are kept until you remove the friendship or delete your info. If you don't pick any moment for 60 days, your display name and all your friendship records are deleted automatically. QR codes created for adding friends are valid for 2 minutes and deleted within a day at the latest. Report records are kept for up to 12 months to review the report and assess repeated cases.

Data that stays on your device: Your past moments, guesses, the percentages you saw, your streak, language preference and occupation are stored only on your own phone; they are never sent to our server and we cannot see them. Your display name is also stored on your phone; if you use the Friends feature, it is additionally sent to our server.

With the "Reset my saved info" option in the App, you can delete the profile information, moment history and streak on your device and your active record on the server at any time. If you have friends, you choose separately: "Delete my friends too" deletes your display name and all your friendship records from the server and removes you from everyone's list; "Keep my friends" keeps your display name and friendship records so your friends can still recognize you. The technical timer that tracks when you can pick your next moment contains no personal data and is not deleted by this action.

5. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the App sends you one "Today's Moment" notification per day, between 11:00 and 20:00 in your local time. These notifications are created and scheduled on your phone; no data is sent to our server for this. You can turn notifications off at any time in your phone's settings.

If you also turn on "Notify me when my moment ends" on the result screen, you will receive a reminder when your current moment ends, at most twice a day and only between 09:00 and 20:00. These notifications are also scheduled on your phone; no data is sent to our server.

6. Sharing

The App opens your phone's share menu so that you can share your result or activity. Sharing happens only when you choose to, through the app you choose; the App collects no additional data in this process.

7. Friends

The Friends feature is optional. To add a friend, two people must be together in person: one shows the QR code in the App, the other scans and confirms it. The person showing the code is considered to agree to the pairing, and the person scanning confirms it. The App has no chat, messaging, follower counts or public profiles.

You can remove a friend from your list at any time; once removed, neither of you can see the other, and the other person is not notified. You can report a friend for an inappropriate display name or disturbing behavior. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours; the reported person is not told who reported them.

8. Where Is Your Data Stored and Who Is It Shared With?

Your data is not shared with or sold to third parties. We use Supabase, a cloud infrastructure provider, to host the data. The servers are located in the European Union, in Germany (Frankfurt, eu-central-1). Supabase acts only as a technical service provider and does not use your data for its own purposes. By using the App and giving consent for optional information, you agree that this data is processed on these servers.

9. Privacy Threshold (Small Group Protection)

If a comparison group has fewer than 20 active users, no percentage is shown for that group. This is designed to prevent individuals in small groups from being identified indirectly.

10. Your Rights

You have the right to learn whether your data is processed, to request information about it, to request its correction or deletion, and to learn whether it is used in line with its purpose. To exercise these rights, contact us at info@nasilogreniyorum.com, or delete your data directly in the App with the "Reset my saved info" option.

11. Age Requirement

The App is not intended for anyone under 18 and does not knowingly collect data from them.
