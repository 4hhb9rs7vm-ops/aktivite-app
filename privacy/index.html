Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026 · Türkçe sürüm

This privacy policy explains what data is processed when you use the "Şu An: Who's Doing What?" mobile app (the "App"), how it is used, and what your rights are.

1. Data Controller

Name: Fatih Mehmet Şenel
Contact: info@nasilogreniyorum.com

2. What Data Do We Process?

The App lets you pick what you are doing right now and compare it, as a percentage, with other users. For this purpose, the following data is processed:

Data Required? Description
The activity or mood you pick Yes Required for the App's core function.
Anonymous user ID Yes A random technical ID created automatically when you first open the App. We never collect your name, email address or phone number.
Country No Used for the comparison filter.
Age range No Used for the comparison filter.
City No Used for the comparison filter (Türkiye only).
Gender No Used for the comparison filter; a "Prefer not to say" option is available.

Before any optional information is sent to our server, the App shows you a separate consent screen. If you do not consent, this information is never sent to the server and only the "World" comparison is available.

The App does not access your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone. No third-party advertising or analytics trackers are used.

3. How Do We Use Your Data?

The data is used only to calculate and show you the share of other users doing the same activity. Your data is never used for advertising, marketing, profiling, or sold to third parties.

4. How Long Is Your Data Kept?

When you pick an activity, your record stays active for 60 minutes and is included in the calculations during that time. Expired records are automatically deleted from the server. Your optional profile information and language preference are stored locally on your device.

With the "Reset my saved info" option in the App, you can delete the profile information on your device and your active record on the server at any time.

5. Today's Moment Notifications

If you allow notifications, the App sends you one "Today's Moment" notification per day, between 11:00 and 20:00 in your local time. These notifications are created and scheduled on your phone; no data is sent to our server for this. You can turn notifications off at any time in your phone's settings.

6. Sharing

The App opens your phone's share menu so that you can share your result or activity. Sharing happens only when you choose to, through the app you choose; the App collects no additional data in this process.

7. Where Is Your Data Stored and Who Is It Shared With?

Your data is not shared with or sold to third parties. We use Supabase, a cloud infrastructure provider, to host the data. The servers are located in the European Union, in Germany (Frankfurt, eu-central-1). Supabase acts only as a technical service provider and does not use your data for its own purposes. By using the App and giving consent for optional information, you agree that this data is processed on these servers.

8. Privacy Threshold (Small Group Protection)

If a comparison group has fewer than 20 active users, no percentage is shown for that group. This is designed to prevent individuals in small groups from being identified indirectly.

9. Your Rights

You have the right to learn whether your data is processed, to request information about it, to request its correction or deletion, and to learn whether it is used in line with its purpose. To exercise these rights, contact us at info@nasilogreniyorum.com, or delete your data directly in the App with the "Reset my saved info" option.

10. Age Requirement

The App is not intended for anyone under 18 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

11. Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. When significant changes are made, the "Last updated" date on this page will change.
