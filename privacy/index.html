2. What Data Do We Process?

The App lets you pick what you are doing right now and compare it, as a percentage, with other users. If you wish, you can also see the current moment of friends you've paired with via a QR code or an invite link. For these purposes, the following data is processed:

About moods: Some mood options, such as "I'm sick", may reveal information about your health. Picking these options is entirely optional. If you pick one, it is processed like any other choice: only with your anonymous user ID, only to calculate percentages, and for no longer than 3 hours. If you have turned on "Let my friends see my moods", the mood you pick is also shown to your paired friends during that time; this option is off by default. By picking such options, you give your explicit consent to this processing.

Before your country, age range, city, gender and university are sent to our server, the App shows you a separate consent screen. If you do not consent, this information is never sent to the server and only the "Everyone" comparison is available.

Camera: The App uses your camera only to scan a friend's QR code in the Friends feature, and only while you have the "Scan code" screen open. Camera images are not recorded or sent to our server; only the content of the QR code is read. The App does not access your location, contacts, photos or microphone. No third-party advertising or analytics trackers are used; usage events are kept only on our own server.

3. How Do We Use Your Data?

Your activity, mood and optional comparison information are used only to calculate and show you the share of other users doing the same activity or in the same mood (for example among all users, in your country, or among students of your university). In the campus comparison, who is at which university is never shown; only the aggregate percentage is shown. Percentages are calculated from aggregated, anonymous counts created every 15 minutes, not from individual records.

Campus League: If you picked your university, the moments you choose contribute to your campus's weekly ranking (for example "the campus that studies the most"). Rankings are published only as aggregate percentages; no user is ever shown by name or individually. Campuses with fewer than a minimum number of participants are not ranked. If you don't want to take part, you can remove your university in the Me tab or withdraw your comparison consent.

If you use the Friends feature, your paired friends see only your display name and your current moment (your activity; your mood only if mood sharing is on). Your past moments, age, city, country, gender, occupation and university are never shown to your friends.

Your data is never used for advertising, marketing, profiling, or sold to third parties.

4. How Long Is Your Data Kept?

When you pick an activity, your record stays active for 3 hours; if you pick a new moment during that time, it replaces your previous record and is included in the calculations during that time. Expired records are automatically deleted from the server.

Friends feature data: Your display name and friendship records are kept until you remove the friendship or delete your info. If you don't pick any moment for 60 days, your display name and all your friendship records are deleted automatically. QR codes created for adding friends are valid for 2 minutes and deleted within a day at the latest. Report records are kept for up to 12 months to review the report and assess repeated cases.

Ask feature data: Your notification address is kept as long as notifications are allowed and is deleted automatically once your phone can no longer receive notifications. Ask records are deleted automatically after 30 days.

Invite data: Invite codes are valid for 24 hours and can be used by up to 10 people; expired invites are deleted within 7 days at the latest. Approved or declined requests are deleted automatically after 30 days, unanswered requests after 7 days, and wrong code attempts within 1 day. Because you share it, the invite link may contain your display name; you decide who you share it with.

Question of the Day, hello and usage data: Question of the Day answers, guessing game guesses, archive answers and hello records are kept for up to 12 months to calculate and show the results of the related series. Usage events are kept for up to 12 months.

Data that stays on your device: Your past moments, guesses, the percentages you saw, your streak, language preference and occupation are stored only on your own phone; they are never sent to our server and we cannot see them. Your display name is also stored on your phone; if you use the Friends feature, it is additionally sent to our server.

With the "Reset my saved info" option in the App, you can delete the profile information, moment history and streak on your device and your active record on the server at any time. If you have friends, you choose separately: "Delete my friends too" deletes your display name, all your friendship records, your notification address, your ask records, your invites and your invite requests from the server and removes you from everyone's list; "Keep my friends" keeps your display name and friendship records so your friends can still recognize you. The technical timer that tracks when you can pick your next moment contains no personal data and is not deleted by this action.

5. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the App sends you one "Today's Moment" notification per day, between 11:00 and 20:00 in your local time. These notifications are created and scheduled on your phone; no data is sent to our server for this. You can turn this notification off at any time in the App under Me → Notifications, or in your phone's settings.

If you turn on "Remind me in an hour", you will receive a reminder one hour after you pick a moment, at most twice a day. If you are waiting to pick a new moment and answer yes to "Want us to tell you when it opens?", you will receive one extra notification when the wait is over. No reminders are sent between 22:00 and 09:00. These notifications are also scheduled on your phone; no data is sent to our server.

"Ask" notifications: In the Friends feature, you can ask a friend who hasn't picked a moment "What are you doing right now?". This notification is sent from our server through the Expo notification service to Apple (APNs) or Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging). For this, your phone's notification address (push token), time zone and app language are stored on the server. The notification only contains the asker's display name. When the asked person picks a moment, the asker gets an "answered" notification. You can ask the same person once every 4 hours, a person can receive at most 3 asks per day, and no asks are sent between 23:00 and 09:00 in the recipient's local time. You can turn off receiving asks at any time with the "Let my friends ask me" switch in the Friends tab.

Announcement notifications: If you allowed notifications, we may send at most one short announcement per day about the App (e.g. the day's question or a new feature) from our server through the same infrastructure. These are sent between 10:00 and 22:00 in your local time and contain no personal information. If you don't want them, turn off Şu An notifications in your phone's settings.

Invite notifications: When someone accepts your invite link or code and becomes your friend, you get a "joined with your invite" notification. These are sent through the same infrastructure as "Ask" notifications, contain only the other person's display name, and are not sent between 23:00 and 09:00 in the recipient's local time. No notification is sent when someone declines an invite.

6. Sharing

The App opens your phone's share menu so that you can share your result or activity. Sharing happens only when you choose to, through the app you choose; the App collects no additional data in this process.

7. Friends

The Friends feature is optional. There are two ways to add a friend. If you are together, one person shows the QR code in the App and the other scans and confirms it; the person showing the code is considered to agree to the pairing, and the person scanning confirms it. If you are apart, you can send an invite link or a 4-character invite code: if the person who opens the link or enters the code accepts the invite, you become friends. Sharing the link or code means you agree to the pairing, so send it only to the person you want to add. An invite is valid for 24 hours and can be used by up to 10 people. If someone you don't want is added, you can remove them from your list at any time. No one can search for or find you by username. The App has no chat, messaging, follower counts or public profiles.

You can remove a friend from your list at any time; once removed, neither of you can see the other, and the other person is not notified. You can report a friend for an inappropriate display name or disturbing behavior. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours; the reported person is not told who reported them.

8. Where Is Your Data Stored and Who Is It Shared With?

Your data is not shared with or sold to third parties. We use Supabase, a cloud infrastructure provider, to host the data. The servers are located in the European Union, in Germany (Frankfurt, eu-central-1). Supabase acts only as a technical service provider and does not use your data for its own purposes. "Ask" notifications are delivered, for transmission only, through the Expo (650 Industries, USA), Apple and Google notification services; no data other than the notification address and the notification text is sent to these services. By using the App and giving consent for optional information, you agree that this data is processed on these servers.

9. Privacy Threshold (Small Group Protection)

If a comparison group (world, country, age, gender, city or campus) does not have enough active users, no percentage is shown for that group. For the Question of the Day, the number of people is shown only once it exceeds 3,000; for smaller groups only percentages are shown. This is designed to prevent individuals in small groups from being identified indirectly.

10. Your Rights

You have the right to learn whether your data is processed, to request information about it, to request its correction or deletion, and to learn whether it is used in line with its purpose. To exercise these rights, contact us at info@nasilogreniyorum.com, or delete your data directly in the App with the "Reset my saved info" option.

11. Age Requirement

The App is intended for people aged 15 and over and does not knowingly collect data from anyone under 15. If you are under 18, you should use the App with the knowledge of your parent or legal guardian. If you notice that data of someone under 15 is being processed, let us know at info@nasilogreniyorum.com and it will be deleted.
