Frequently Asked Questions

Why do I see "You're one of the first" instead of a percentage?
To protect privacy, a percentage is shown only when a comparison group has at least 20 active people. When there are fewer, the percentage appears automatically once it's available.

Do I need an account?
No. The app never asks for your name, email address or phone number.

How long does my pick stay active?
Your activity stays active for 60 minutes and is then automatically deleted from our server.

How do I delete my info?
Use "Reset my saved info" at the bottom of the home screen to instantly delete the info on your device and your active record on the server.

How do I turn off Today's Moment notifications?
You can turn off notifications for Şu An in your phone's settings.

How do I change the language?
Use the "Türkçe · English" option at the very bottom of the home screen.
